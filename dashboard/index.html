Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next?

Every protocol in the sealed DefiLlama snapshot that still has no token, ranked by what its launch would be worth if it priced the way its already-trading category peers price today — each with the concrete steps to farm it before it issues one.

How the radar values a launch

The implied value is calibrated from the snapshot itself. For each category, take the protocols whose token already trades (at least $1M of TVL behind the market cap), and compute the median of market cap ÷ TVL — the price the market actually pays per dollar of TVL in that category. Each peer's ratio is capped at 5 first (beyond that, a price reflects float and listing scope, not TVL), a thin category is shrunk toward the snapshot-wide median so two peers cannot shout (peer multiple = (n × category median + 3 × global median) / (n + 3)), and the result is held inside 0.2×–3× the global median so no single thin category can dominate the board.

Each pre-token protocol is then scored by one function of its own record: implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity, where growth = 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +100%) × 0.6 and maturity = 0.70 + 0.30 × min(days running with no token, 1095) / 1095. A protocol that is growing and has operated longer without a token ranks above an identical one that is shrinking or brand new.

Every row shows its own factors and a confidence grade: high when its category has at least eight trading peers and the record is complete, medium with at least three peers and at most one missing figure, low otherwise. Discount low-confidence rows; the snapshot speaks thinly about them.

What the board excludes, and why

Protocols whose token already trades are never ranked by the model — they sit in the “Live tokens” view at the market's own values. Centralised exchanges are excluded. So are versioned deployments whose name ends in a release marker (V2, v3, 1.5): they are pools of another protocol and inherit its token story. Also excluded: wrapped products of an asset that already trades (a name carrying “Wrapped”, or a bare ticker standing in a bridge category — the WBTC record is the wrapped asset itself, not a launch), products of a listed exchange group, and a tokenless record that shares its name with a record that already carries a token. What remains is ranked whole — the watchlist of protocols above $50M TVL is the floor of the board, never its ceiling.

Honesty box

An implied value is this model's claim about relative worth, not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when, or what it will trade at. Farm plans are generic playbooks derived from each protocol's category, chains, size and age — check a protocol's own site for any live points programme before acting, and never pay anyone to “claim” an airdrop. Figures come from one sealed snapshot; they do not move after its date.
