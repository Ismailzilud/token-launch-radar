Token Launch Radar

Protocols that record no token yet, ranked by what a token would be worth if their record stayed as it is — and how to farm each one before it exists.

How a value is derived

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth × plausibility

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap / TVL of protocols in the same category whose token already trades in this snapshot, shrunk toward the global median when a category has few traded peers. No name is scored and nothing is hardcoded: change the snapshot and every multiple moves.
  • Growth — the seven-day TVL change at half weight, clamped. Maturity — years tracked with no token recorded, full weight at three years. Breadth — a small step per chain, capped at twenty.
  • Plausibility — a discount, never a deletion, for shapes that rarely launch a token of their own: exchanges, canonical bridges, wrapped or staked products of a token that already trades, versioned successors. The reason is printed on the row.
  • Already trading — protocols whose token exists are never ranked. They are the yardstick: open any row to see the traded peers its multiple came from.
