Token Launch Radar

Protocols that record no token yet, priced by a curve fitted to the ones that do — each with a launch odds, a farm verdict in dollars, and the model's own out-of-sample grade printed next to it.

How a price is built

implied value = size curve(TVL) × category adjustment × launch odds × growth × breadth

  • Size curve — a power curve of market cap against TVL, fitted by least squares in log space on every protocol in this snapshot whose token already trades. The category adjustment is the median amount its traded peers beat or miss that curve by.
  • Launch odds — of the protocols DefiLlama has tracked for about as long as this one, the share that record a token today, shrunk toward the snapshot-wide share and blended with the same share in its category. A cross-section of the snapshot, not a prophecy.
  • Set aside, not ranked — exchanges, canonical bridges, wrapped or staked forms of tokens that already trade, and versioned successors never launch a token of their own, so they leave the board entirely; they are listed below with the reason, not buried at a discount. The watchlist is never set aside.
  • Farm verdict — the farmer pool is taken as 8% of the implied value; a deposit earns its share of that pool against the capital expected to chase it (1% of TVL). Net of estimated fees and a 4% T-bill yield on the deposit: FARM when the slice clears twice the costs, THIN when it merely clears them, SKIP when it does not.
